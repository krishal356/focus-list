How to use FocusList

FocusList is your little productivity room.

Keep tasks in the center, reminders on your Sticky Board, and move work through the Kanban Flow. Everything stays saved in this browser.

✍

Create Tasks

Add tasks from the center with a title, priority level (High, Medium, Low), and optional due date and time.

🔍

Search & Filter

Quickly find tasks with live search, status tabs (All, Active, Completed), and priority filters.

📌

Sticky Board

Keep quick thoughts on colored sticky notes. Drag and drop notes to reorder them anytime.

☀

Today's List

See what is due today at a glance. Click any item to jump straight to it in the central workspace.

⇄

Kanban Flow

Move work across TODO → IN PROGRESS → DONE by dragging and dropping cards or using the selector.

✎

Edit & Delete

Click Edit to modify task titles or priorities, or delete tasks when plans change.
